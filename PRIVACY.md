# Privacy Policy – Phitter

**Last updated: August 23, 2025**

Phitter is a free and open-source project, developed without commercial intent, and available publicly at:  
- Web App: [https://phitter.io/](https://phitter.io/)  
- Core Kernel (open source): [https://github.com/phitter-hub/phitter-kernel](https://github.com/phitter-hub/phitter-kernel)  
- Python Package Documentation: [https://docs-phitter-kernel.netlify.app/](https://docs-phitter-kernel.netlify.app/)  

---

## Data Collection
Phitter **does not collect, store, or share any personal data**.  
The application runs locally in the user’s browser or device and does not require login, registration, or access to sensitive information.

---

## Use of Information
Since no data is collected, there is no processing, analysis, or sharing of personal information.

---

## Third-Party Services
Phitter does not integrate advertising, analytics, or any third-party services that track users or process personal data.

---

## Children’s Privacy
Phitter does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users. As no personal data is gathered, the app is safe for all audiences.
